Sift privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Sift is a personal mail app used only by its owner. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

What Sift accesses

With the owner's permission (Google scope gmail.modify), Sift reads Gmail messages and labels in the owner's own accounts and changes labels to sort mail into inboxes. It shows mail to the owner in the app.

How the data is used

Only to sort, display and manage the owner's own mail. Sorting rules run on the owner's own computer. Rules written in plain English are judged only by language models running on the owner's own hardware. Email content is never sent to third-party or cloud AI services.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens are kept in the device keychain. A small log of sorting decisions (message ids and rule names) is kept on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared, transferred to third parties or used for advertising. Sift's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deletion

Signing out removes tokens from the device. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
